Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Quantize is built to need as little of your data as possible — especially given that photos can be personal. This policy explains what happens to your images, what information is collected automatically when you visit the Site, and how third-party advertising and analytics providers use that information.

1. What happens to the images you process

Every image you drop in is read directly into your browser's memory and processed there using your browser's built-in canvas — resizing, compressing, converting, and zipping all happen locally. None of it is ever transmitted, uploaded, or stored on a server. Closing the tab discards everything instantly.

2. Information collected automatically

Like virtually all websites, our hosting provider automatically logs basic technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request — for security and operational purposes. We also use the cookies and tracking technologies described below.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

The Site uses a small number of cookies and browser storage items:

  • Functional: your light/dark theme preference, stored in your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics cookies, used to understand overall traffic patterns — which pages get visited and roughly how many people use the Site.
  • Advertising: if the Site displays ads, Google and its advertising partners may set cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites, and to measure ad performance.

None of these cookies have access to your photos or anything you process, since that information never leaves your browser in the first place.

4. Advertising — Google AdSense

This Site may use Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info. For more detail on how Google uses data when you use our Site, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Google Analytics

Quantize uses Google Analytics to understand overall traffic patterns. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking site-wide using Google's browser add-on, or block it with most ad-blocking extensions. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google Analytics handles data.

6. Accounts and data storage

There is no account system. We don't store your images, filenames, or any identifying information, because none of it is ever sent to us in the first place.

7. Your rights (GDPR — EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you're located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of any personal data we hold about you, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest (such as analytics). Because we collect only minimal automatically-logged technical data and don't maintain accounts, there is very little personal data to act on — but you can contact us at any time using the details below, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Your rights (California — CCPA/CPRA)

If you're a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to request deletion of that information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, which under California law can include the use of advertising cookies for personalized ads. You can exercise the opt-out described in Section 4 above, or contact us directly to make a request. We do not sell personal information in the traditional sense — we have no customer data to sell — but the law treats certain advertising-cookie sharing as a "sale," which the opt-out links above address.

9. Children's privacy

Quantize is not directed at children under 13 and is not knowingly used to collect personal information from anyone, of any age, since no personal information is collected through use of the tool itself. If you believe a child has provided us personal information (for example, by email), please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-party links

The Site links to other sites we operate (such as Quiet Zone) and may display links or ads to third-party sites. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of sites we don't operate — please review their own privacy policies.

11. Data security

Because images are never transmitted or stored on our servers, there's nothing for a server-side breach to expose. Automatically-logged technical data (Section 2) is handled by our hosting provider under industry-standard security practices.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes — like adding a new category of data collection — will be described plainly here.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests relating to your data, can be sent to hello@quantizeapp.io.
